        About & sources

        Leonida Sunset is an offline, open guide for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. It runs entirely from its own files: it makes no network request, saves your checklist in this browser's localStorage, and exports it as a JSON file you can import back.

        Where the facts come from

        • Official trophy names, descriptions and grades — Exophase: Grand Theft Auto VI trophies
        • Grades and the counts PlayStation tracks (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone race locations, 9 activity types, 18 meditation spots, 52 Leonida areas, 9 gangs, 8 garage properties, 100 vehicles, 100 WAiNK locations, 3 card sets, 50 challenges, 250 stunts, 20 races, 5 bank vaults, 10 Scooter Bros vehicles, $250,000 laundering, $100,000 spent, 500 choices) — PSNProfiles: Grand Theft Auto VI trophies
        • Every per-trophy guide is written for this entry, in our own words, from the official description. A guide that repeats the description adds nothing, so each one says what to do, how, and what to watch for — and says plainly where a step was not verified in-game.

        How the roadmap and missables were built

        Only from the official descriptions: which chapters and missions (Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory, the final mission), which stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret and 8 strands in all), and which collectible counts. Nothing here rests on an unverified location or an unplayed step.

        Your progress

        Ticking a trophy saves it in this browser only. Export downloads a JSON file of your earned trophies; import reads that file back. “Load sample progress” ticks a sample of 13 trophies so you can see the tracker working — it is a demo, not earned play, and Reset clears it.
